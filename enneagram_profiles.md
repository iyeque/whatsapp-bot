# Enneagram Personality Profiles

## Type 1: The Perfectionist
Type 1s are principled, purposeful, and self-controlled. They strive for improvement and have high standards for themselves and others. They are ethical, responsible, and often feel a strong sense of mission.

## Type 2: The Helper
Type 2s are caring, generous, and people-pleasing. They are driven by a need to be loved and needed. They are empathetic, warm, and supportive but may neglect their own needs while focusing on others.

## Type 3: The Achiever
Type 3s are success-oriented, pragmatic, and adaptive. They excel at setting and achieving goals. They are efficient, image-conscious, and motivated by a fear of failure or worthlessness.

## Type 4: The Individualist
Type 4s are sensitive, introspective, and expressive. They value authenticity and seek meaning in their experiences. They are creative, emotionally honest, and often feel different from others.

## Type 5: The Investigator
Type 5s are perceptive, innovative, and cerebral. They seek knowledge and understanding to feel secure in the world. They are independent, analytical, and often need time alone to recharge.

## Type 6: The Loyalist
Type 6s are committed, security-oriented, and vigilant. They anticipate problems and work to create safety. They are responsible, trustworthy, and value loyalty in relationships.

## Type 7: The Enthusiast
Type 7s are spontaneous, versatile, and optimistic. They seek new experiences and avoid pain or limitation. They are fun-loving, adventurous, and bring positive energy to situations.

## Type 8: The Challenger
Type 8s are powerful, decisive, and self-confident. They protect themselves and those they care about. They are assertive, straightforward, and take charge in challenging situations.

## Type 9: The Peacemaker
Type 9s are receptive, reassuring, and agreeable. They seek harmony and avoid conflict. They are accepting, patient, and good at seeing multiple perspectives.
